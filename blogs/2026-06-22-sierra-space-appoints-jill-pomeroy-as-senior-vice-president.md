LOUISVILLE, Colo. – June 22, 2026 – Sierra Space Corp. (“Sierra Space”), a defense-tech space company delivering solutions for the nation’s most critical missions and advancing the future of security in space, today announced that Jill Pomeroy has joined the company as senior vice president of Government Relations.
