LOUISVILLE, Colo. – June 30, 2026 – Sierra Space Corp. (“Sierra Space”), a defense-tech space company delivering solutions for the nation’s most critical missions and advancing the future of security in space, announced today the appointment of Jeff Schrader as chief financial officer.
