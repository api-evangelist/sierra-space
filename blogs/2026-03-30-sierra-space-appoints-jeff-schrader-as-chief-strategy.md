LOUISVILLE, Colo. – March 30, 2026 – Sierra Space Corp. (“Sierra Space”), a proven defense-tech space company delivering solutions for the nation’s most critical space missions and advancing the future of security in space, today announced that Jeff Schrader has joined the company as chief strategy officer.
